{\LARGE \textbf{\color{DeepBlue} Product Requirements Document}}\\
{\Large Multi-Lender Student Financing Marketplace}\\
\textit{Status: MVP built and tested \quad Owner: Arnav \quad Version: 1.0}

\section*{1. Problem Statement}
Students seeking education financing must currently approach lenders one at a time, with no way to compare eligibility or terms upfront. This creates two problems: students waste time applying to lenders they don't qualify for, and they have no way to know whether they're getting a competitive rate before committing.

\section*{2. Goal}
Let a student enter their financial profile once and immediately see every partner lender they're eligible for, ranked by estimated cost, with a clear reason given for any lender they don't qualify for.

\section*{3. Personas}
\textbf{Primary: The Applying Student.} Needs to borrow between Rs.~50K and Rs.~25L for tuition, often has thin or developing credit, and doesn't know which lenders will even consider their application.

\textbf{Secondary: The Partner Lender.} Wants qualified leads only --- applicants who already meet their underwriting criteria, reducing wasted review effort on their side.

\section*{4. User Stories}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=18pt, itemsep=3pt]
\item As a student, I want to enter my loan amount, credit standing, and desired tenure once, so that I don't have to repeat this for every lender.
\item As a student, I want to see which lenders I'm eligible for and which I'm not, with a clear reason for rejection, so I don't waste time on a dead-end application.
\item As a student with thin credit, I want to know if adding a co-signer would unlock additional lenders, so I can make an informed decision before applying.
\item As a student, I want offers ranked by estimated interest rate, so the best available option is obvious without manual comparison.
\item As a product owner, I want every step of this journey logged as an event, so we can see where applicants drop off and improve the funnel.
\end{itemize}

\section*{5. Functional Requirements}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=18pt, itemsep=3pt]
\item The system shall accept an applicant's requested loan amount, credit tier, requested tenure, and co-signer availability.
\item The system shall evaluate the applicant against all active partner lenders' underwriting rules (minimum credit tier, loan amount range, maximum tenure, co-signer conditions).
\item The system shall return a ranked list of eligible offers, sorted by estimated interest rate (ascending), and a separate list of ineligible partners with a specific, human-readable rejection reason for each.
\item The system shall log an analytics event at each of the four funnel stages: eligibility checked, offers viewed, partner selected, application initiated.
\item The system shall expose a funnel summary endpoint returning real, queried conversion counts at each stage.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{7. Success Metrics (KPIs)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=18pt, itemsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Eligibility-to-offers-viewed rate} --- did the applicant see any real offers, or hit a dead end?
\item \textbf{Offers-viewed-to-partner-selected rate} --- of those with eligible offers, how many engaged further?
\item \textbf{Partner-selected-to-application-initiated rate} --- the hardest conversion: intent to action.
\item \textbf{Co-signer unlock rate} --- how often a co-signer prompt converts an otherwise-ineligible applicant.
\end{itemize}

\section*{8. MVP Scope}
\textbf{In scope:} 4 simulated partners with distinct, realistic underwriting rules; real-time eligibility and rate-ranking logic; full funnel event instrumentation; a queryable funnel analytics endpoint.

\textbf{Out of scope for MVP:} real partner API integrations, actual credit bureau pulls, document upload and verification, loan servicing post-approval.

\section*{9. Roadmap (Post-MVP)}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=18pt, itemsep=2pt]
\item Replace simulated partner rules with real partner API contracts.
\item Add a soft credit-check integration to replace self-reported credit tier.
\item A/B test the co-signer prompt's placement and wording to improve the unlock rate.
\item Add segment-level funnel breakdowns (by credit tier, requested amount band) to the analytics endpoint.
\end{enumerate}
